## Local rules

Your own rules, appended to the generic ones when `orca-coordinator render` builds
COORDINATOR.md. Examples of what belongs here: the label your team puts on
sub-tickets, a prefix every automated review must carry, a reviewer to request on
every PR, channels where the bot cannot post, house conventions the generic rules do
not know. Keep each rule to one line; the weekly eval may propose edits here too.
